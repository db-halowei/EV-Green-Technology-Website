All about BYD

BYD- Build Your Dreams


BYD, which stands for “Build Your Dreams,” is a Chinese high-tech company founded in 1995 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China. The company initially focused on rechargeable batteries before expanding into automobiles, renewable energy, electronics, and rail transit. BYD entered the automotive industry in 2003 and has since become a major manufacturer of electric and new-energy vehicles. The company is known for developing its own battery and EV technologies, including the Blade Battery and e-Platform 3.0. Today, BYD operates globally and offers a wide range of electric and new-energy vehicles, including models available in Malaysia such as the DOLPHIN, ATTO 3, SEAL, and SEALION 7.
