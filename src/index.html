Things to know about EV

What is an Electric Vehicle (EV)?

An electric vehicle (EV) is powered entirely or partially by electricity instead of gasoline or diesel. EVs utilize an electric motor, a battery system, and advanced electronics to provide propulsion. They have emerged as a viable solution for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on fossil fuels.

Why EV vehicles?

6 reasons why you must own an EV vehicle

Cost

Electricity is generally more affordable in comparison to petrol or diesel, so each kilometer driven costs less, hence EV vehicle owners are less affected by sudden spikes in global oil prices.

Eco Friendly

No tailpipe emissions is produced, helping reduce air pollution and greenhouse gases. Moreover, their batteries can be recycled thus reducing waste pollution.

Long Range

Modern batteries give long driving ranges, letting drivers travel far without frequent charging and making them reliable for day to day commutes.

Convenient Charging

EVs can be charged anywhere whether charging it overnight at home or using public fast charging stations is malls, highways and work places.

Lower Maintenance Cost

They need less servicing, with no oil changes, fewer moving parts, and brakes that last longer, cutting overall maintenance costs.

Government Incentives
