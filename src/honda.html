Honda Background


Honda Motor Co., Ltd. is a Japanese automotive company founded by Soichiro Honda in September 1948 in Hamamatsu, Japan. The company initially focused on producing motorcycles and later expanded into automobiles and power products. Honda entered the automobile industry in the 1960s and gradually expanded its manufacturing and business operations around the world. Today, Honda is known for developing motorcycles, automobiles, and various power products while continuing to invest in new technologies. As the automotive industry moves toward electrification, Honda is also developing electric and other electrified vehicles as part of its future mobility strategy.
