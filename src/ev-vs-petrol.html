Electric and petrol compared

Two ways to power the same journey.

Compare everyday ownership before looking at the detailed specifications below.

Electric

Efficient, quiet and ready to charge at home.

Electric power offers smooth response with fewer routine powertrain service items.

  • Lower energy cost
  • Home charging
  • Fewer powertrain maintenance items
  • No tailpipe emissions

Petrol

Familiar, flexible and quick to refuel.

Petrol vehicles use an established nationwide network and familiar servicing support.

  • Fast refuelling
  • Widespread fuel stations
  • Convenient long-distance travel
  • Familiar servicing infrastructure

Detailed comparison

Practical differences at a glance.

Comparison between electric and petrol vehicles.
Factor Electric Petrol
Energy Electricity stored in a battery Petrol burned by an engine
Running cost Often lower per kilometre Depends heavily on fuel price
Maintenance Fewer moving powertrain parts Engine oil and more mechanical parts
Tailpipe emissions No tailpipe exhaust while driving Produces exhaust emissions
Long journeys Charging stops may require planning Fuel stations are widely available
Refill at home Possible with suitable home charging Normally requires a fuel station
